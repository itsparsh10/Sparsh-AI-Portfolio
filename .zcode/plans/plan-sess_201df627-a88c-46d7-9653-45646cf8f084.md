- Headings `#08090a`, body `#3f444c`, muted `#71767f`
- **Acid lime `#e4f222` unchanged** — the single primary CTA (lime fill, dark text); supporting accents green/red/violet for badges only
- Inter Variable (next/font) with `cv01/ss03/zero` features, weights 300/400/510/590 (no 700+), tracking -0.022em on display
- JetBrains Mono for technical metadata; radii: cards 12px / buttons 6px / pills 9999px / badges 4px
- Max-width 1200px, section gap 96px, all tokens scoped under `.rigel-page` (light island, immune to site dark theme)
